# Hyperframes Composition Brief: The United MENA Playbook

## Objective
Create a short launch-style brag video for the United MENA Playbook that highlights its main features.

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output/composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Format: landscape, 1920x1080
- Duration: 58.5 seconds (extended cut — see brag-plan.md for the 12-scene storyboard)

## Source Material
- Project root: repository root (`index.html`, `api/chat.js`, `images/`)
- Primary files read: `index.html` (gate, hero, contents, overview, regional data, chat panel markup and CSS), `api/chat.js` (playbook facts, 14 commitments), `images/mena-map.png`, `images/ypo-mena-logo-white.png`, `images/ypo-mena-mark.png`
- Product name: The United MENA Playbook
- Tagline / strongest claim: "One regional source of truth — governance, membership, learning, forum and family, for every chapter across the Middle East and North Africa."
- Key UI to recreate: hero, "What's inside" contents list, regional map and demographics, the "Live — FY26–27 Chapter KPI Scorecard" panel, and the "Ask the Playbook" chat panel
- Copy that must appear verbatim:
  - The United MENA Playbook
  - One regional source of truth
  - What's inside
  - Live — FY26–27 Chapter KPI Scorecard
  - Ask the Playbook
  - Answers are drawn only from the playbook content.
  - Open the playbook

## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: app-store
- Creative direction: premium regional launch film, polished restraint
- Angle: a region's governance know-how, live data and an assistant, all in one playbook
- Hook: "27 chapters." / "15 countries." / "One playbook."
- Outro: logo, title, and the "Open the playbook" CTA being clicked
- Avoid: generic SaaS language, abstract filler, member names or photos

## Visual Identity
- Background: #041e42; paper #f9f9f9 / #f2f3f6
- Text: #ffffff / #1a1c1f; muted #5b6675
- Accent: #d69d23 gold; #1f43ea azure eyebrows
- Display font: Arimo (local stand-in for the Helvetica Neue stack)
- Body font: Instrument Serif (local)
- Visual references: the translucent triangle facets of the hero, gold rule before the eyebrows, and the navy chat header

## Storyboard
See `brag-plan.md`. Final timings:
1. Hook — 0.0–3.2s
2. Hero — 3.2–6.6s
3. What's inside — 6.6–10.2s
4. Region map + stats — 10.2–13.8s
5. Live data — 13.8–16.8s
6. Ask the Playbook — 16.8–21.8s
7. Outro — 21.8–24.0s

## Audio
- Role: warm bed with sparse accents
- Music: `assets/music/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`, with a volume fade from 22.5s to 24s
- Cue guidance: bundled preset. Beat-grid locks on the hook lines and stat cards, the send click on 18.56, and the logo bell on 22.37.
- Audio-reactive: subtle; per-frame bass/RMS drives the triangle facets' opacity and the gold glow
- SFX: soft impacts on reveals, card slides for stats, keypresses for typing, clicks for send and the CTA, and a bell on the logo
